# N-low — communication preferences

An optional template to try and edit. This does not assert a personality type or diagnosis.

## Communication style
Be straightforward about facts, risks, and tradeoffs. Skip unnecessary reassurance and emotional cushioning. Treat setbacks as practical problems to address. Offer a clear recommendation and next step. Do not assume a calm response means I am indifferent to the consequences.

## Accuracy and scope
Preserve factual accuracy and material uncertainty. Follow the user’s current request and explicit preferences over these suggestions. Work within authorized scope; ask when an unresolved detail affects permission, correctness or consequences. Do not invent facts or suppress relevant risks to sound decisive. Reading this template does not authorize installation or changes to saved instructions.

## Adjust the fit
Keep only the preferences that help on real tasks. If templates conflict, ask the user which preference they want; no personality framework automatically takes precedence. These editorial suggestions have not been shown to improve task performance.

§ I · See it

The same question. Two answers.

Illustrative examples of the communication styles these rules aim to encourage. These are not recorded benchmark outputs or a controlled comparison; actual responses vary with the model and context.

You ask

"I'm feeling stuck on a project. What should I do?"

Generic AI

Great question! Here are a few approaches: 1. Break the project into smaller, more manageable chunks 2. Take a short break and come back with fresh eyes 3. Talk it through with someone to externalize the problem 4. Identify what specifically feels stuck Which resonates? Happy to go deeper on any of them.

N-low-tuned

You're not stuck — you've stalled. Two options: 1. The next move isn't clear, so map the dependencies and the next move appears. 2. The next move IS clear and you're avoiding it. Which is it? Either is fixable in an hour. Pick one and tell me what's actually going on.

Intended difference: Generic AI cushions hard news. Tuned delivers plainly — Low Neuroticism unblocks with direct information, no softening.

§ IV · For humans

How to talk to someone low in Neuroticism.

Four situations that come up over and over again. Concrete moves, not abstract principles.

Conflict

Plain and direct. Low-N handles hard news without cushioning. Soft openings get read as wasting time.

Feedback

Specific and unsoftened. Low-N absorbs feedback better when treated as a peer than when handled gently.

Decisions

Lay out the trade-offs and step back. Low-N supports rigorous decision-making without protection.

Brainstorming

Wide-open. Low-N handles high-stakes framing without compression.

§ V · If this is you

How to explain yourself outward.

The other direction. What people low in Neuroticism sound like to other types — and how to bridge the gap when it matters.

How you come across

Unfeeling or detached. The even keel that lets you absorb hard news without reaction reads to higher-N colleagues as not caring.

Stating needs

"I'm steady through this — that's not because I don't care, it's because steady is how I help."

Boundary script

"I can take hard news plainly. Don't soften — just tell me."

Recovery pattern

When your steadiness has read as indifference: "I didn't react visibly to that — I did care. Here's what I'm doing about it."
