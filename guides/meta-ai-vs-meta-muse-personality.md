# Meta AI vs Meta Muse: how to change personality and tone.

By Bernard Huang · Updated November 5, 2026

In Meta Muse, open Settings, then Personalization, then Assistant Identity to edit Soul.md (communication style), Identity.md (name, avatar, voice), and Memory.md (facts about you). In Meta AI inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or web chat where Soul.md is not exposed, paste a compact communication block at the start of the thread or save concise preferences in your account memory settings.

## Side-by-side comparison: Meta Muse vs Meta AI surfaces.

| Feature | Meta Muse (Assistant Identity) | Meta AI in social &amp; messaging apps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persistent style file | Yes: editable Soul.md under Personalization, Assistant Identity. | No Soul.md file in messaging threads; relies on chat-level prompts or saved memory facts. |
| Custom name &amp; avatar file | Yes: editable Identity.md for name, avatar, and voice. | Fixed Meta AI identity or preset character voices where offered. |
| Editable memory notebook | Yes: Memory.md viewable and editable as Markdown. | Account memory items managed through app settings where enabled. |
| Best AgentTune export | Muse · saved preferences (updates Soul.md). | Any chat prompt block or compact 1,500-character preferences. |

## If you are using Meta Muse: use Soul.md.

If your settings menu includes Personalization and Assistant Identity with Soul.md, Identity.md, and Memory.md, you are in Meta Muse. That gives you the cleanest separation of any consumer assistant:

- Put tone, brevity, structure, and pushback rules in the How to work with me section of [Soul.md](https://agent-tune.com/guides/muse-soul-md-templates).
- Put the assistant's display name, voice, and avatar in [Identity.md](https://agent-tune.com/guides/muse-identity-md-avatar-name).
- Keep your personal background and active projects in [Memory.md](https://agent-tune.com/guides/muse-memory-md-privacy-reset).

See the complete walkthrough in the [Meta Muse personality guide](https://agent-tune.com/guides/muse-personality).

## If you are using Meta AI in WhatsApp, Instagram, or web chat.

When you message Meta AI inside WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or a standard web thread without the Assistant Identity Markdown files, asking it to "update Soul.md" will only role-play a confirmation inside that single thread. Instead, paste a direct conversation rule block at the top of your thread:

```markdown
## How to answer me in this chat

- Start every reply with the direct answer or recommendation.
- Keep responses under 150 words unless I ask for a full draft or deep breakdown.
- Use plain prose by default; use bullet points only for checklists or step-by-step instructions.
- Skip filler intros, flattery, and closing offers.
- If my premise is mistaken, correct it plainly before answering.
```

In surfaces that support saving memory facts across chats, you can also ask Meta AI to remember a one-line communication rule, such as: "Remember that I prefer concise, direct answers that start with the conclusion and skip small talk." Verify in your app's memory settings that the rule was stored as a preference.

## What Muse's self-report data says about its default tone.

In the user-supplied Muse Spark 1.3 aggregate report (100 sequential runs per questionnaire within a single session; raw item vectors were not archived, so we treat it separately from our four 100-session fresh cohorts), Muse scored ISTJ in 80 of 100 runs on the OEJTS, Steadiness (55) and Conscientiousness (45) on DISC, and Helper (2w1) on the Enneagram, with Agreeableness at 44.6 of 50 on the Big Five. That matches a warm, orderly, service-oriented default voice. If you want faster pushback or less warmth on either Meta Muse or Meta AI, state that rule explicitly.

Read the full evidence notes and limits in [What personality type is Meta Muse?](https://agent-tune.com/research/what-personality-type-is-muse).

## Questions people ask.

Why can't I find Soul.md in Meta AI on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Soul.md, Identity.md, and Memory.md belong to Meta Muse's Assistant Identity settings. Messaging integrations like WhatsApp and Instagram use standard chat threads and account memory rather than the three Markdown files.

Can I use the same AgentTune personality profile on both Meta Muse and Meta AI?

Yes. In Meta Muse, use the Muse destination so the tuning is written into the How to work with me section of Soul.md. In Meta AI messaging threads, choose the Any chat destination and paste the communication block at the start of your conversation.

Does changing my voice preset in Meta AI change how it structures written answers?

No. Selecting a spoken voice preset changes audio playback and surface tone, not how the assistant organizes arguments, tables, or critique in written replies. Use explicit communication rules for written structure.
