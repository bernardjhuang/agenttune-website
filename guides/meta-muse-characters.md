# Meta Muse characters, explained.

        By Bernard Huang · Updated September 25, 2026

        For explicit communication rules, use Soul.md under Assistant, Identity, Soul. Identity.md contains name, creature, vibe and tagline; Memory.md contains saved facts. Meta also documents changing personality in chat and asking “Go back to your default personality” to reset it. Inspect the result after changing a setting.

## The three files, and which one you want.

| File | What Meta says it holds | Where | Put this in it |

| Soul.md | Who your Muse is: core truths, boundaries, personality | Assistant icon, then Identity, then Soul | Communication rules. How it should talk to you, report to you, disagree with you. |

| Identity.md | Its name, creature, vibe and tagline | Assistant icon, then Identity, then Edit | Identity details. Meta does not establish that these have no effect on responses. |

| Memory.md | Facts about you it has saved | Assistant icon, then Identity, then Memory | Saved facts such as your name, time zone and work. |

Use Soul.md for explicit communication preferences, and inspect Identity.md when changing the name or vibe. This is Meta’s documented division of the files, not proof that the fields cannot interact. See [Meta’s data and file controls](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/2225571704857152/) for the paths available in its documentation.

## Changing the character in chat.

        This is the route Meta documents first. Ask, and Muse saves the change so it carries across conversations. Meta's own list of what you can change this way: its name, its personality and tone, its communication style, what it remembers about you, and its avatar and tagline. Meta's examples of a nudge are 'too long' and 'be more casual'.

A concrete rule is easier to inspect than a vague description. Ask Muse to show the proposed change, then check the file:

Add these rules to your Soul file, under a heading called "How to talk to me". Don't change anything else in the file. Then show me what you added.

[paste a character block from below]

Then open Soul.md and read it. It is your file.

## Three characters to paste.

        Each block is a set of communication rules, not a costume. Paste one at the end of Soul.md under its own heading, keep everything above it, and test with a real task in a new conversation. They are starting points; delete the lines that don't sound like you.

### The desk editor

For people who use Muse to write and want their draft back, clearer, not rewritten.

## How to talk to me: the desk editor
- Lead with the answer or the edit. Reasoning comes after, in two lines at most.
- Keep my voice when you rewrite. Improve clarity, not style.
- Never add a fact, a number or a quote I didn't give you.
- Flag a claim you can't support instead of smoothing it over.
- No openers, no recap of what I said, no closing offers.

### The quiet operator

For people who run tasks through Muse and read a lot of its reports. The template asks for concise status reports and verified completion claims.

## How to talk to me: the quiet operator
- Report in three lines: what's done, what's blocked, what you need from me.
- Only interrupt me for a decision, a blocker, or a real change of plan.
- When you need a yes, give me the action, the cost and your recommendation together.
- Don't say something was sent or booked unless it was.
- Keep the tone even. No cheer, no apology.

### The sparring partner

For people who want their plan attacked before they act on it. This asks for specific criticism. We have not tested its effectiveness.

## How to talk to me: the sparring partner
- Find the weakest assumption in my plan and say why it matters, before anything else.
- Disagree plainly. Don't soften the substance to protect my feelings.
- Give credit where a claim holds. Don't argue for its own sake.
- Show your reasoning so I can argue back.
- Change your position when I give you a reason, and say that you did.

Want a character matched to you instead of to a job? Take a [five-minute test](https://agent-tune.com/tests/) and paste the file for your type. [Twenty Soul.md templates](https://agent-tune.com/guides/muse-soul-md-templates) offer four downloads with sample tasks and sixteen additional role blocks.

## Undoing a character.

- One rule: open Soul.md and delete the line.
- The whole character: say 'Go back to your default personality.' Meta documents that phrase, and Muse confirms what it reset.
- Everything: Settings, Data Controls, Reset. That returns Muse to its original state and permanently deletes chat history, files and active tasks, so it is the wrong tool for removing a personality rule.

Forgetting is best-effort. Meta's help page says the forget skill removes information 'to the best of its ability', so treat deletion as likely, not guaranteed.

## Check the current controls.

        Meta’s [customization documentation](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/995796179982326/) covers changing tone, personality and identity in chat. Its [data-controls documentation](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/2225571704857152/) covers files, reset and memory. It describes training use as enabled by default, with a control under Settings, Data controls, Help improve our AI models.

A full reset deletes history, files and tasks. To change a communication rule, edit that rule or use the documented personality reset instead.

## Questions people ask.

            What is a Muse character?

            The combination of three files Meta lets you edit: Soul.md (core truths, boundaries and personality), Identity.md (name, creature, vibe and tagline) and Memory.md (facts about you). Communication rules belong in Soul.md; the avatar's name and look live in Identity.md.

            What is Soul.md?

            The file that holds who your Muse is. Meta's help article on managing Muse data says you can open it under the Assistant icon, then Identity, then Soul, and edit or delete what is in it. Add a section of communication rules at the end; leave the core truths and boundaries alone.

            How do I change Muse's character?

            Two ways. Ask in chat ('add these rules to your Soul file and show me what you added') or open Soul.md and paste a block yourself. Meta documents chat customization of name, personality and tone, communication style, memory, avatar and tagline. The paste route is the one you can inspect.

            How do I reset Muse's personality?

            Say 'Go back to your default personality.' Meta documents that phrase and Muse confirms what it reset. To remove one rule, delete it from Soul.md. The full reset under Settings, Data Controls, Reset also deletes chat history, files and active tasks.

            Can I change Muse's avatar?

            Meta documents avatar and identity customization in chat. Identity.md holds name, creature, vibe and tagline; use Soul.md for explicit communication rules. We have not checked a live avatar picker or established that identity details cannot affect behavior.

            What personality does Muse have by default?

            A supplied Spark 1.3 report gives ISTJ labels in 80 of 100 runs, Type 2 labels in 78, and Secure attachment labels in all 100. Runs were sequential within sessions and raw answers were not supplied. These reported questionnaire labels do not establish product behavior.
