How to change ChatGPT's voice on iOS, Android, and desktop.

  1. Via Settings: Open the ChatGPT app → tap your Profile / Menu → Settings → Voice → preview all nine voices and save.
  2. Mid-conversation: While in Voice Mode, open the voice settings/sliders control (or simply say “Switch your voice to Cove”) to swap voices on the fly.
  3. Fix long spoken monologues: Voice Mode inherits your Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions. Add the concise spoken-cadence block below so ChatGPT stops reading out 90-second bullet lists while you drive or walk.
## Voice & Spoken Cadence Rules - Answer in 2–4 natural spoken sentences first; wait for me to ask before going deeper. - Never read out raw URLs, markdown syntax, or long nested bullet lists aloud. - Give one clear recommendation first instead of an unranked list of five options. - Push back plainly if my premise has a flaw; skip praise and closing offers.

Questions people ask.

Which ChatGPT voices are female and which are British?

Juniper, Maple, Sol, and Vale have female-leaning registers (Vale has a British accent). Arbor, Breeze, Cove, Ember, and Spruce have male-leaning registers (Arbor has a British accent).

Why does ChatGPT Voice Mode talk for too long?

By default, ChatGPT answers in written-essay structure with bulleted lists and closing offers. Adding a 2–4 sentence spoken-cadence rule in Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions shortens replies across both voice and text.

Do ChatGPT Personality presets (Cynic, Robot, Listener, Nerd) affect Voice Mode?

Yes. Your account-wide Base style and tone preset and Custom Instructions apply to new Voice Mode conversations, though you should test changes in a fresh voice chat.

Can I change ChatGPT's voice on the web or only on mobile?

You can switch voices in Settings → Voice across the ChatGPT iOS, Android, desktop, and supported web clients.
