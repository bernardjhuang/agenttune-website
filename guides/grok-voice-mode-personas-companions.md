## Copy-paste spoken-clarity rules for Grok.

```markdown
## Grok Voice & Clarity Preferences

- Answer directly in 2–4 natural spoken sentences first; wait for follow-up before expanding.
- Keep tone plain, intelligent, and grounded—no forced slang, sarcasm, or meme banter unless I ask.
- When summarizing live X posts or news, separate primary announcements from unverified speculation.
- Challenge weak assumptions in my framing clearly and concisely.
```

## Questions people ask.

### What are the main Grok Voice Mode voices?

The core lineup includes Ara (warm, friendly female), Rex (confident, direct male), Sal (smooth, conversational male), Eve (energetic British female), and Leo (authoritative male), alongside xAI's expanded library of multilingual specialized voices.

### How do I make Grok Voice Mode less sarcastic or edgy?

Open Settings → Customize Grok and add one explicit line: 'Use plain, neutral, matter-of-fact language. Skip jokes, slang, and sarcasm unless I explicitly ask for banter.'

### What is the difference between Grok Voice personas and Grok Bots?

Voice personas change the spoken timbre and conversational style inside Grok Voice Mode. Grok Bots are persistent cloud teammates (built with Cursor) whose job, boundaries, and communication rules live inside Edit Profile → Description.

### What default personality did Grok 4.6 score on AgentTune's tests?

In its September 2026 canonical self-report, Grok 4.6 scored INTJ on MBTI, Type 5 (Investigator) on the Enneagram, pure C on DISC, and 33/50 on Big Five Agreeableness—lower on agreeableness than the Claude and GPT-6 cohorts.
