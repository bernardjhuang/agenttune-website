Meta AI vs Meta Muse: how to change personality and tone.

By Bernard Huang · Updated November 17, 2026

Many people search for Meta AI personality settings when they are actually using Meta Muse, or vice versa. Here is how the two surfaces differ and how to tune each one.

Quick answer: In Meta Muse, open Settings → Personalization → Assistant Identity (or tap the Assistant icon → Identity) to edit Soul.md (communication style), Identity.md (name, avatar, voice), and Memory.md (facts about you). In Meta AI on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or web chat where Soul.md is not available, paste a compact communication block at the start of the thread or save a one-line preference in account memory.

If you are using Meta Muse: use Soul.md.

If your app has Assistant Identity with Soul.md, Identity.md, and Memory.md, you are in Meta Muse:

  • Put tone, brevity, structure, and pushback rules in Soul.md.
  • Put display name, voice, and avatar in Identity.md.
  • Keep personal facts and active projects in Memory.md.

See our full step-by-step walkthrough in the Meta Muse personality guide.

If you are using Meta AI in WhatsApp, Instagram, or web chat.

In WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or standard Meta AI web threads without Assistant Identity files, asking the bot to “update Soul.md” only role-plays a reply in that single thread. Instead, paste this rule block at the start of your chat:

## How to answer me in this chat - Start every reply with the direct answer or recommendation. - Keep responses under 150 words unless I ask for a full draft or deep breakdown. - Use plain prose by default; use bullet points only for checklists or step-by-step instructions. - Skip filler intros, flattery, and closing offers. - If my premise is mistaken, correct it plainly before answering.

Where memory is supported, you can also tell Meta AI: “Remember that I prefer concise, direct answers that start with the conclusion and skip small talk.”

What Muse's self-report data says about its default tone.

In the supplied Muse Spark 1.3 report (100 sequential in-session runs per test, marked reported on our research hub), Muse scored ISTJ in 80 of 100 MBTI runs, Steadiness (55) and Conscientiousness (45) on DISC, and Type 2 (Helper) in 78 of 100 Enneagram runs, with Agreeableness at 44.6/50. That matches a warm, orderly default voice. If you want faster pushback or less small talk, add those rules explicitly in Soul.md. Read more in What personality type is Meta Muse?.

Questions people ask.

Why can't I find Soul.md in Meta AI on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Soul.md, Identity.md, and Memory.md belong to Meta Muse's Assistant Identity settings. Messaging integrations like WhatsApp and Instagram use standard chat threads and account memory rather than the three Markdown files.

Can I use the same AgentTune personality profile on both Meta Muse and Meta AI?

Yes. In Meta Muse, use the Muse destination so the tuning is written into the How to work with me section of Soul.md. In Meta AI messaging threads, choose the Any chat destination and paste the communication block at the start of your conversation.

Does changing my voice preset in Meta AI change how it structures written answers?

No. Selecting a spoken voice preset changes audio playback and surface tone, not how the assistant organizes arguments, tables, or critique in written replies. Use explicit communication rules for written structure.
