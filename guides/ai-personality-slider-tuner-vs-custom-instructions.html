Why do checkable rules matter so much? In our 60-reply controlled pilot, replacing default prompting with concrete behavioral rules lifted perfect 5/5 answer-first openings from 14/20 to 19/20, cut hedging phrases from 0.35 to 0.05 per reply, and reduced unsolicited follow-up questions from 0.90 to 0.25 without hurting accuracy.

Where to paste your tuned prompt in 30 seconds.

  • ChatGPT: Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions → paste into Box 2 (guaranteed under 1,500 chars).
  • Claude: Settings → Profile → Personal preferences (Claude guide).
  • Meta Muse / OpenClaw: Paste into Soul.md / SOUL.md (Muse · OpenClaw).
  • Google Gemini: Settings & help → Personal Intelligence → Instructions for Gemini (Gemini guide).
  • xAI Grok: Settings → Customize Grok (Grok guide).
  • Coding Agents: ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md, ~/.codex/AGENTS.md, or .cursor/rules/ (7-agent matrix).

Questions people ask.

Is this AI personality tuner free and private?

Yes. All six sliders and prompt exports run 100% locally in your browser tab—no email, no login, and nothing is sent to a server.

Will the generated prompt fit inside ChatGPT's 1,500-character Custom Instructions limit?

Yes. Selecting ChatGPT Custom Instructions shows a live character counter and outputs a compact ~550–850 character block that leaves plenty of room for your own work context.

Should I use sliders or take an MBTI / Big Five / DISC personality test?

If you already know how you want your AI to talk, sliders are faster. If you want to discover blind spots in your work style or communication triggers first, try our free no-email DISC (3 min), MBTI (5 min), or Big Five (7 min) tests.

How do I know if the slider prompt actually improves replies?

Save your new prompt, start a fresh chat, and run two real work prompts before and after—or score the two outputs blindly in the AgentTune Instruction A/B Test Bench.
