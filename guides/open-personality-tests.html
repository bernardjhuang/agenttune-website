Questionnaires, scores and their limits.

By Bernard Huang · Updated September 26, 2026

Use the IPIP Big Five adaptation for current local scoring, or choose communication preferences directly. Other framework pages explain availability and link to the original publisher. A questionnaire score is not a diagnosis, proof of an AI’s inner personality or evidence that a tuning improves performance.

Current availability

The IPIP Big Five adaptation is available with public-domain items. OEJTS and ODAT have noncommercial license conditions; OEPS needs exact license clarification; ECR-R has research permission distinct from product and redistribution rights. Those four questionnaires are excluded from the current bundle.

Read the per-instrument rights record. A software MIT license does not relicense question text.

Big Five scoring

Complete all 50 items using the versioned response scale. Reverse keyed items use 6 minus the response. Each dimension sums ten scored items (10–50); its average is the total divided by ten (1–5). Invalid values are rejected and missing responses produce no final score.

Current results contain no population percentiles, reference indices or automatic template selection. Neuroticism reverses the direction of IPIP Emotional Stability; Openness corresponds to Intellect/Imagination. The specification records the adaptation.

Historical research scoring

Published studies used legacy adaptations and, in some cases, deterministic tie-break labels. MBTI item 1 and two Enneagram items had undocumented substitutions. Preserve those edition identities when interpreting the results. Raw vectors and numerical results remain unchanged; restricted item wording in public downloads is replaced by stable item labels.

Choose behavior explicitly

Questionnaires are optional. Use the preference builder to request the detail, warmth, structure and pushback you want. Review each rule before saving it. No framework or score outranks your explicit request, accuracy or authorized scope.

Questions people ask.

Are all five questionnaires MIT licensed?

No. Original AgentTune templates and code have their own license; instrument content has separate terms. Review the rights record before reuse.

Does the Big Five result show a population percentile?

No. It reports raw dimension totals and item means. The former reference constants had no verified normative sample.
