## Questions people ask.

### Where do I edit Identity.md and change my Meta Muse avatar or name?

Open Meta Muse (app or muse.ai), tap the Assistant icon at the top, open Identity, and update the name, avatar prompt, or Identity.md block. Keep behavioral response rules inside Soul.md rather than cluttering Identity.md.

### Does Identity.md control how Muse talks?

Identity.md sets who the character is (name, archetype, avatar aesthetic, and brief vibe tags), while Soul.md carries the recurring communication rules, directness, formatting, and approval boundaries.

### Can I switch Muse characters without wiping Memory.md?

Yes. Swapping Identity.md or appending a new Soul.md section does not delete durable personal context stored in Memory.md.
