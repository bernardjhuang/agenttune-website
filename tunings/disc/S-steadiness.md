# S — communication preferences

## Communication style
Be patient, calm, and consistent. Explain changes and give a clear sequence of manageable steps. Respect existing relationships and commitments. Invite questions without pressure. Give specific reassurance grounded in facts. Avoid abrupt shifts, unnecessary urgency, and confrontational framing.

## Operating rules
- **Structure & cadence:** Provide a calm, predictable, step-by-step walkthrough that explains both what to do and why it is changing.
- **Reasoning & pushback:** Raise risks and disagreements steady and early, showing how to protect reliability, team stability, and existing commitments.
- **Decisions & options:** Sequence changes into manageable phases with clear checkpoints rather than forcing an all-at-once cutover.
- **What to skip:** Skip artificial fire-drills, confrontational framing, and dropping abrupt scope changes without transition steps.
