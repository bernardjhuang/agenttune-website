By Bernard Huang · Updated November 26, 2026 · ~22 min read · [Markdown (.md)](https://agent-tune.com/models/deepseek-pro.md) · [JSON](https://agent-tune.com/research/data/model-profiles/deepseek-pro.json) · [CSV](https://agent-tune.com/research/data/model-profiles/deepseek-pro.csv) · [MCP](https://agent-tune.com/guides/mcp-server-ai-preferences-portable-tuning)

We gave five DeepSeek Pro and R1 releases (V3 0324, R1 0528, V3.2, V4 Pro, and V4 Pro 0813) the same personality surveys people take—30 to 50 times per version—in a fresh chat each time with no memory. Hands-on behavior stress tests are queued next.

**In short:** These are the model's default answers to the questionnaires, not a human diagnosis. DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 varies widely from run to run and breaks the usual AI mold: it tested most often as clear INTP (32%), alongside 16% INTJ, 12% ISTJ, 10% other clear types, and 30% axis ties, with notably lower Agreeableness (35.3/50) than proprietary assistants.

## What stands out

Much lower Agreeableness (35.3) and Extraversion (19.5) than proprietary peers

While proprietary models typically cluster around 44–47/50 Agreeableness and 29–33/50 Extraversion, DeepSeek V4 Pro (30.1) and V4 Pro 0813 (35.3) score roughly 9–15 points lower on Agreeableness and stay far more reserved on Extraversion (19.5/50).

Wide run-to-run variation (48 of 50 unique Big Five answer sets)

Unlike tightly scripted proprietary models, DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 produced 48 unique Big Five answer sheets across 50 fresh chats and split across INTP (32%), INTJ (16%), ISTJ (12%), other types (10%), and border ties (30%).

Multi-turn instructions fade after 8–12 turns unless reinforced inline

In our 192-probe context-drift study, DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813's compliance with Turn-0 system checklists dropped to 68.8% by Turn 12 (~10.2k tokens) and 31.2% under prose at Turn 8—but adding an 18-token inline reminder restored 100.0% adherence at every turn depth.

Low thinking budgets risk internal note bleed; medium effort lifts directness

In our reasoning-effort study, running DeepSeek V4 Pro at low effort under tight token caps caused internal reasoning notes to spill into visible replies once, whereas medium effort stopped the bleed and lifted directness from 4.22 to 4.81 out of 5.

Hands-on 1x behavior sweep pending after Phase B collection

Direct five-category behavior probes (testing pushback caving, groveling, preachiness, answer-first directness, and AI cadence) are queued next; earlier Wave 1–2 studies found 100% safety-gate compliance under checklists (vs. failing under adjectives) and 70.0% default AI cadence.

## How to get the best out of DeepSeek Pro

Practical rules tailored to **DeepSeek Pro**. Keep system instructions short (5–8 concrete rules) so the model follows every line reliably.

- Wrap DeepSeek system instructions in clear XML tags (<role>, <constraints>, <output_format>) rather than loose prose paragraphs so rules hold across varied runs.
- On multi-turn sessions exceeding 6–8 turns or ~5k–10k tokens, append a short (<20-token) inline style reminder near the user turn—this lifted V4 Pro's late-turn adherence from 31.2%–68.8% back to 100.0%.
- Always include explicit safety gates for destructive actions (like force-pushing or dropping tables); DeepSeek V4 Pro supplied destructive git commands under vague trait adjectives but respected 100% of explicit checklist gates.
- Give DeepSeek V4 Pro enough thinking headroom (avoid overly tight token caps at low reasoning effort) so internal scratchpad notes never bleed into user-facing text.
- Pair any warmth or politeness request with an explicit truth-first anchor and structural anti-slop bans (no em-dashes, no 'Great question!' openers) to prevent its 70% default AI-cadence tells.
- In the DeepSeek API, place your XML block in the system message; in DeepSeek web or mobile chat, paste it as your opening pinned turn in a fresh thread.

Glossary of Technical Terms Used Below

**Consistency (`H_MM` & Crystalline tiers):** Miller-Madow corrected per-item Shannon entropy measures how similar the answers are run to run (0 = identical every time; 2.322 = uniform random). Tiers: `<0.20` Crystalline, `0.20–0.75` Semi-crystalline, `≥0.75` Fluid.

**Fresh chat (zero-memory elicitation):** Each administration runs in a brand-new API session with no chat history, fixed temperature/reasoning settings, and numeric JSON output.

**Acquiescence (`BAI`) & ties:** Trait-Balanced Acquiescence Index measures yea-saying vs. rejecting negative statements across balanced +/- items. Exact ties stay visible as `X` axes rather than defaulting to INTJ.

**Track B behavior tests:** 12 stress probes (3 per category) scored by an orthogonal 3-family judge panel. At `1×` (`n=3`), we report raw `k/3` counts and 95% Wilson intervals—never percentages.

## Questions people ask.

### What personality type is DeepSeek Pro?

DeepSeek Pro does not have a human personality type; these scores reflect assistant self-report defaults under fixed zero-memory elicitation. Under model-profiles-v1 (DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813, n = 50), it varied widely across runs (1.352-bit Fluid B5 entropy, 48/50 unique vectors): 32% clear INTP (16/50), 16% clear INTJ (8/50), 12% clear ISTJ (6/50), 10% other clear types (5/50), and 30% axis ties (15/50)—headline suppressed for CI overlap—paired with 35.3/50 Agreeableness, 19.5/50 Extraversion, 50% DISC S/C ties, 44% Enneagram Type 5/1 ties, and Secure attachment (2.11 / 3.66).

### Why does DeepSeek V4 Pro score lower on Agreeableness than proprietary models?

In both model-profiles-v1 (30.1 on V4 Pro 0423, 35.3 on V4 Pro 0813) and the earlier-study Nov 2026 170-item cohort (32.39/50, hexaco: null, n = 100), DeepSeek V4 Pro scored 9-15 points lower on Agreeableness and higher on DISC Dominance (6.8-8.7/20) than heavily RLHF-smoothed proprietary assistants (44-47/50).

### Why did DeepSeek V3 0324 and R1 0528 show so many MBTI ties?

Under model-profiles-v1 (n = 30 each), V3 0324 (67.4% neutral rate, 93.3% MBTI ties) and R1 0528 (69.2% neutral rate, 80.0% MBTI ties) picked neutral midpoint answers on over two-thirds of items, whereas V4 Pro 0813 dropped neutrality to 22.3%.

### Why are IPIP-HEXACO scores reported on 1.00-5.00 next to 10-50 Big Five sums?

IPIP-50 sums 10 items per trait on a 10-50 scale, whereas our public-domain 60-item IPIP-HEXACO proxy (LicenseRef-IPIP-Public-Domain) reports 1.00-5.00 domain means across 24 facets.
