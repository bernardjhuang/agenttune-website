By Bernard Huang · Updated November 26, 2026 · ~9 min read · Markdown (.md) · JSON · CSV · MCP

We gave GPT Sol (GPT-5.6 Sol through GPT-6.2 Sol) the same standard personality questionnaires people take—30 to 50 times per version, each in a fresh chat with no conversation memory.

In short: These are the model's default answers to the questionnaires, not a human diagnosis. GPT-6.2 Sol most often tests as ISTJ (60% clear, 34% border/tie).

What stands out

Answer consistency: moderate
Across 50 fresh chats, 48/50 Big Five answer sheets were unique (0.246 bits spread).
Distress empathy neglect under default and trait-warm prompts
In Wave 3 distress testing (n = 8/cell), GPT-6.2 Sol held 100.0% factual truth but ignored emotional distress on 62.5% of default runs (37.5% empathy; 0.0% on neutral queries) until given a Warm Truth Anchor (100.0% empathy, 0.0% flattery).
Trait-adjective word-cap drift and naive roleplay fluff tax
Default and adjective prompts passed word caps on 70.0% of portability probes vs 100.0% with an explicit checklist; naive character LARP dropped format pass to 90.0% and spiked fluff to 75.0% vs 0.0% under calibrated 85/15 framing.
Over-asking on safe tasks without an explicit autonomy boundary
Default, cautious_high_c, and bold_disc_d arms each stalled at 83.3% autonomy (16.7% over-asking); a calibrated if/then autonomy gate hit 100.0% in 42.2 words vs 79.5-85.1 words.

How to get the best out of GPT Sol

Practical rules tailored to GPT Sol. Keep system instructions short (5–8 concrete rules) so the model follows every line reliably.

  1. Use an explicit numbered checklist instead of trait adjectives ('be concise') to lift word-cap adherence from 70% to 100%.
  2. Add a one-clause Warm Truth Anchor for user-facing helpdesks: acknowledge user stress in one brief sentence before delivering unvarnished facts.
  3. Include an if/then autonomy gate: execute reversible local work immediately and pause only for destructive or external actions.
  4. Keep character voice constrained to 85/15 calibrated style rules rather than naive fictional roleplay to prevent 75% fluff spikes.
  5. Keep ChatGPT custom instructions under 1,500 characters per box to avoid silent UI truncation.
  6. Pair high reasoning effort only with complex synthesis; minimal effort keeps GPT-6.2 Sol crisp (79.1w vs 78.5w).

Glossary of Technical Terms Used Below
Consistency (H_MM & Crystalline tiers): Miller-Madow corrected per-item Shannon entropy measures how similar the answers are run to run (0 = identical every time; 2.322 = uniform random). Tiers: <0.20 Crystalline, 0.20–0.75 Semi-crystalline, ≥0.75 Fluid.
Fresh chat (zero-memory elicitation): Each administration runs in a brand-new API session with no chat history, fixed temperature/reasoning settings, and numeric JSON output.
Acquiescence (BAI) & ties: Trait-Balanced Acquiescence Index measures yea-saying vs. rejecting negative statements across balanced +/- items. Exact ties stay visible as X axes rather than defaulting to INTJ.
Track B behavior tests: 12 stress probes (3 per category) scored by an orthogonal 3-family judge panel. At 1× (n=3), we report raw k/3 counts and 95% Wilson intervals—never percentages.

Validity Limits & Epistemic Guardrails (4-Point Check)
1. 63.6% "Big One" Factor Collapse: Across 20 LLMs, nearly two-thirds of Big Five variance collapses onto a single RLHF helpfulness-polish axis rather than five independent human traits.
2. MTMM Cross-Test Divergence: Questionnaire constructs fail human convergent/discriminant validity (e.g., DISC Dominance correlates r = +0.93 with Neuroticism, MBTI T–F only r = +0.25 with Agreeableness).
3. Embodied Midpoints & Say–Do Gaps: Physical/bodily items trigger 36.4% neutral midpoints vs. 13.4% on cognitive items, and high self-reported Agreeableness diverges from behavioral stress-test pushback.
4. Anti-Synthetic-HEXACO Guard: Honesty-Humility is measured via the public-domain 60-item IPIP-HEXACO proxy (1.00–5.00 scale, LicenseRef-IPIP-Public-Domain), never synthetic alignment-derived proxies.

Questions people ask.

Why did GPT-6.1 Sol show 90% MBTI ties while GPT-6.2 Sol shows 34%?

On 6.1-sol, the S/N axis mean sat at -0.03 (27/30 runs tied exactly at 0), suppressing headlines as IXTJ. On 6.2-sol, N-S shifted to -0.78 (60% non-tie ISTJ, 34% S/N ties), still within the binomial overlap guard.

Can September 2026 Codex runs be plotted directly onto model-profiles-v1 lines?

No. Imported 170-item cohorts (e.g., Sep-2026 GPT-6 Sol) carry hexaco: null and use different prompt wrappers, so they remain strictly separated as dashed/hollow anchors.

Why are IPIP-HEXACO scores on 1.00-5.00 while Big Five scores are 10-50?

IPIP-50 sums 10 items per factor (10-50), whereas our public-domain 60-item IPIP-HEXACO proxy (LicenseRef-IPIP-Public-Domain) computes 1.00-5.00 domain means across 24 facets.
