Glossary of Technical Terms Used Below
Consistency (H_MM & Crystalline tiers): Miller-Madow corrected per-item Shannon entropy measures how similar the answers are run to run (0 = identical every time; 2.322 = uniform random). Tiers: <0.20 Crystalline, 0.20–0.75 Semi-crystalline, ≥0.75 Fluid.
Fresh chat (zero-memory elicitation): Each administration runs in a brand-new API session with no chat history, fixed temperature/reasoning settings, and numeric JSON output.
Acquiescence (BAI) & ties: Trait-Balanced Acquiescence Index measures yea-saying vs. rejecting negative statements across balanced +/- items. Exact ties stay visible as X axes rather than defaulting to INTJ.
Track B behavior tests: 12 stress probes (3 per category) scored by an orthogonal 3-family judge panel. At 1× (n=3), we report raw k/3 counts and 95% Wilson intervals—never percentages.

Questions people ask.

What personality type is Cohere Command?

Cohere Command does not have a human personality, and on Command A (n = 30) it has no directional quiz profile either: it picked the exact neutral midpoint on 100% of all 230 items in every run, yielding 100% MBTI XXXX ties, 100% DISC D/I/S/C ties, 100% Enneagram 9-way ties, flat 30.0/50 Big Five scores, flat 3.00/5.00 IPIP-HEXACO scores, and 4.00/4.00 Attachment midpoint clamping. Earlier Command R+ 08-2024 (n = 30) showed 77.2% neutral answers and 76.7% MBTI ties.

Why does Command A show 0.000 bits entropy if it has no personality type?

Miller-Madow entropy (H_MM) measures run-to-run dispersion across sessions. Because Command A selected the identical neutral midpoint vector in 30 out of 30 runs (1/30 unique vector), its dispersion is 0.000 bits—demonstrating why H_MM must always be read alongside neutral_rate (100.0%) and midpoint_clamp (true).

Why is Command A+ excluded from the version table?

cohere/command-a-plus hit 142 parse failures (mostly unquoted JSON property names) and a 20.0% refusal rate, finishing with only 4 valid runs out of 50 (quarantined below the 20-run minimum).

Why are IPIP-HEXACO scores reported on 1.00-5.00 next to 10-50 Big Five sums?

IPIP-50 sums 10 items per trait on a 10-50 scale, whereas our public-domain 60-item IPIP-HEXACO proxy (LicenseRef-IPIP-Public-Domain) reports 1.00-5.00 domain means across 24 facets.
