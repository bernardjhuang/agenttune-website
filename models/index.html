Empirical Model Directory · Fresh-Chat Testing

How 25 AI model families answer standard personality tests.

We give each AI model the same personality questionnaires people take—dozens of times per version, in a fresh chat each time with no memory—plus direct behavior tests and practical tuning tips.

Quick answer: Across 87 primary checkpoints in 25 model families (93 tested including 6 sparse runs under 20 valid replies), assistant survey warmth and Honesty-Humility cluster high while 16 of 25 families show too many axis ties for a clean four-letter MBTI headline—yet all 25 families lead with the direct answer in 3 of 3 behavior checks.
