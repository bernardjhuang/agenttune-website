Most AIs think alike.

Personalization goes a long way.

Across six frontier models — Claude, GPT, Gemini, GLM, Grok, MiniMax — and 2,200 personality tests, they all defaulted to the same voice: strategic, blunt, model-first. Until you tell an AI how you think, you're talking to the same character everyone else is.

The big stat
99.5% INTJ

597 of 600 MBTI runs returned INTJ across the six frontier models we tested. The remaining three landed one axis away. None went anywhere else.
