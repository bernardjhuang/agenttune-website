FAQ

Short answers to the obvious questions.

What's a "tuning file"?

A Markdown file matched to your personality. Paste it into your AI agent's system prompt and the agent's interaction style extends with how you process — less translation, sharper alignment, fewer cycles spent reformatting answers in your head.

Browse the library to preview any tuning file with a sample response.

Is the free tier actually any good?

Yes. The 43 free tuning files are exactly what we use as the source material for the Premium master file. If you stack a few (e.g., your MBTI + Enneagram + a couple of OCEAN dimensions), the result gets you roughly 80% of the way to Premium.

Premium adds: synthesis into one file, conflict resolution where systems disagree, deeper integration guides, and items outside the public library.

What's actually in the Premium master file?

One Markdown file, ~600–1200 words, including:

  • Your synthesized personality across all 5 instruments
  • Cross-instrument conflict resolution (e.g. when MBTI E and Big Five low-E disagree)
  • Custom-tuned phrases and rules for your specific combination
  • A short "operator's manual" for your agent — what to do, what to avoid, when to ask
  • A versioned changelog so you can see how the file evolves if you re-tune

You own it. It's yours forever, and you can edit, share, fork, or paste it into anything.

Which agents does this work with?

Any agent with a system prompt slot. Confirmed and paste-ready:

  • Claude — Claude Code (CLAUDE.md), Claude.ai (Project Instructions)
  • ChatGPT — Custom Instructions, ChatGPT Projects
  • OpenAI Codex CLI — AGENTS.md in project root
  • Cursor — .cursor/rules/*.mdc
  • Gemini — Gems, Code Assist, Antigravity
  • Codex CLI — AGENTS.md in project root
  • Anything else — paste at the start of any conversation

Take any free test to get the exact paste-ready snippet for each surface.

Why does default AI feel so generic?

Because every frontier model defaults to roughly the same personality. We tested 6 frontier models (Claude, GPT-5.5, Gemini, Grok, GLM, MiniMax) on the MBTI 100 times each — 597 of 600 came back INTJ. Useful baseline. One out of sixteen, though, and it rarely matches the way you actually work.

The full study: /research.

Do you store my data?

Free tier: nothing leaves your device. Tests run client-side; tunings are static files. We don't collect anything.

Premium tier: we store your raw assessment responses encrypted, generate your master file, send it to you, and delete the raw responses 90 days after delivery. Your finalized master file stays available at your private permalink. We never sell, share, or train on your data — full stop.

Can I cancel? Refund policy?

Premium is a one-time $9, not a subscription — there's nothing to cancel. If it doesn't measurably improve how your agent talks to you, email us within 30 days for a no-questions refund. The files you've already generated are still yours.
