## How it works & common questions.

These rules change how an AI structures and delivers replies—they don’t change its base reasoning power. Pick your app or preference above to update the text, or paste your current prompt into the [Instruction Checker](https://agent-tune.com/tools/instruction-checker) to spot conflicts.

### Where do I paste ChatGPT custom instructions on web and mobile?

Open `chatgpt.com` or the ChatGPT mobile app, go to Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions, and paste your rules into How would you like ChatGPT to respond? Each box holds up to 1,500 characters.

### How do ChatGPT Projects interact with account-wide Custom Instructions?

Inside a ChatGPT Project, Project Instructions override your account-wide Custom Instructions whenever two rules conflict. Keep a short 6–10 line voice block in global settings and put project-specific schemas or repo rules inside the Project.

### Do I need to take a personality test to use this generator?

No. The default Build with toggles mode builds rules from 12 starting archetypes and 16 plain-English behavior toggles. Or switch to Load 1 of 43 personality types to preview all 43 MIT templates across MBTI, Enneagram, DISC, Attachment, and Big Five without taking a test.

### Are my generated instructions or edits sent to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser tab. Drafts stay in `localStorage` for up to 7 days (click Reset to clear them), and share links only save your toggles and dropdown picks—never your edited text, personal context, or quiz answers.

## The fine print: character limits & why concrete rules beat adjectives.

### Compact ChatGPT vs Full Markdown output.

ChatGPT Personalization caps each box at 1,500 characters. When you pick ChatGPT above, the generator outputs a compact rule block (~300–900 characters) that fits cleanly. Picking Claude, Codex, Cursor, or Markdown gives you the full rule set with headings and safety boundaries.

| Instruction style | Example phrase | Observed effect on model behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vague adjective | “Be helpful, concise, and smart.” | Defaults stay intact: hedges and trailing follow-up questions persist. |
| Behavioral constraint | “Lead with the recommendation. Skip disclaimers. No trailing questions.” | Enforces format directly (our 60-reply pilot lifted answer-first openings from 14/20 to 19/20 and cut hedges from 0.35 to 0.05 per reply). |
