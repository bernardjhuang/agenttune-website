## How to use How to work with me card

- Fill in how you prefer to communicate, receive feedback, make decisions, and get unstuck.
- Click Build my card, then copy or download the Markdown for your team README or AI instructions.
- Optional: check the confirmation box to generate a shareable URL (your card text is encoded directly in the URL hash, not stored on a server).

## A concrete example

“Give me the recommendation first. When you disagree, name the assumption you would change. For decisions, show the top two tradeoffs.”

## Questions and limits

### What does a share link contain?

Only the card text you wrote (up to 2,400 characters), encoded inside the URL `#card=` fragment. Do not include private or confidential details in a shared link.

### Can I change or revoke a shared card?

Because the card text lives inside the link itself rather than a database, anyone holding an old link can still read that version. Create a new link when you update your card.

### Does the card define my personality?

No. It is a practical user manual for how you like to collaborate on work tasks.

## The fine print: research notes & developer API

## Why one personal README works for both humans and AI

What a new teammate needs on week one mirrors what ChatGPT, Claude Code, or `AGENTS.md` needs on turn one: answer-first order, how to deliver critique, when to ask before acting on irreversible changes, and how to flag uncertainty. Keep human READMEs in [Notion](https://agent-tune.com/guides/notion-ai-custom-instructions-agent-persona) or Slack to ~200 words and keep the AI instruction block to 6–10 crisp bullets.

For developers and agents: [tool catalog (JSON)](https://agent-tune.com/resources/tools/catalog.json), [local API documentation](https://agent-tune.com/resources/tools/README.md), and [reusable JavaScript functions](https://agent-tune.com/resources/tools/core.js).
