How to use the communication preferences builder

  1. Choose how much detail you want, your tone, and how you like feedback.
  2. Select the destination and build your instructions. Edit the result in your own words.
  3. Save a copy of your existing instructions, then paste the new preferences at the intended scope. Try a few real tasks and revise.

A concrete example

Prefer brief answers → “Start with the answer. Keep routine replies brief; expand when the task needs explanation.”

Questions and limits

Do I need an MBTI or Enneagram type?

No. This builder starts with preferences you choose directly. It does not infer a personality type or diagnose you. If you already use a type, the original type-based generator is still available.

Will these instructions work with every model?

They are plain-language requests, not guaranteed controls. Models and products can interpret them differently. Test the same task before and after changing your instructions.
