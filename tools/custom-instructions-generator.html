How it works.

These are editable communication preferences, not a way to increase a model’s abilities. Test them on your own tasks and revise anything that does not fit. Personality templates are optional editorial suggestions.

Changing an app, destination or preference regenerates the instructions and resets edits. The share link includes your selected preferences or template and destination, never your edited text or test answers. Anyone with the link can see those selections.
