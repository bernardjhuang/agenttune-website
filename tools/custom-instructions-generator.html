How it works & common questions.

These are editable communication preferences, not a way to increase a model’s underlying reasoning abilities. Changing an app, destination, or preference regenerates the output; audit existing prompts for contradictions anytime in the Instruction Checker.

Where do I paste ChatGPT custom instructions on web and mobile?

On chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT iOS/Android app, open your Profile menu, tap Personalization (or Customize ChatGPT), enable Custom Instructions, and paste your generated rules into “How would you like ChatGPT to respond?” Each field enforces a 1,500-character limit.

How do ChatGPT Projects interact with account-wide Custom Instructions?

Project-level instructions take precedence inside that Project whenever two rules conflict. Keep a compact 6–10 line communication block in global Custom Instructions and place repo, client, or schema rules inside the Project (full precedence guide).

Do I need to take a personality test to use this generator?

No. The default My communication preferences mode builds clean rules directly from four behavioral toggles. Switching to A personality template lets you preview all 43 MIT-licensed tunings across MBTI, Enneagram, DISC, Attachment, and Big Five without taking a test.

Are my generated instructions or edits sent to a server?

No. Everything runs client-side in your browser. Drafts stay in localStorage for up to 7 days (Reset clears them immediately), and share links encode only dropdown selections—never your edited text or test answers.
