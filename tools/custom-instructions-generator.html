How it works.

Each of the 43 tuning files describes communication preferences: pacing, directness, decision framing, and feedback. The generator adds a short model-specific brief and the right format for your destination. Preview the full prompt before copying, then edit it after pasting. ChatGPT custom instructions use a compact version under 1,500 characters; other destinations keep the full tuning.
