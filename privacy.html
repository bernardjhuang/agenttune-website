Privacy Policy

Effective May 28, 2026

The short version: AgentTune needs almost none of your data. All five personality tests and all ten browser tools run inside your browser tab—your answers and pasted instructions never reach our servers. Optional analytics only load if you click Allow, and we never sell data or train models on it.

1. Who we are

AgentTune (“we”, “us”) runs agent-tune.com and the open-source AgentTune template library. Contact us anytime at hello@agent-tune.com.

2. What stays in your browser

Tests and browser tools: All 5 personality tests (Tests) and all 10 tools (Free tools) score and process text locally in your browser tab. Your quiz answers, memory exports, and custom instructions are never uploaded to AgentTune or sent to an AI model.

Local 7-day drafts: Unfinished quiz answers and generator drafts stay in your browser’s localStorage for up to 7 days so you can resume where you left off. Finishing a test, clicking Reset/Clear, or clearing browser storage deletes them immediately.

Share links: If you create a share link in the How to Work With Me card tool, the card text is placed in the URL’s #card=... hash fragment. That text is never stored on our servers, and the page strips the hash from the URL before any analytics script can see it. Because anyone with the link can read it, never put passwords or private keys in a shared card.

3. How we use aggregate data

  • To reply when you email us;
  • To see which pages and tools are useful in aggregate so we can improve them.

4. Service providers

We do not sell personal data or train AI models on visitor data. We use two infrastructure providers:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the static site (Cloudflare Pages) and provides cookieless aggregate web traffic counts.
  • Google Analytics — optional, consent-gated usage counts (only loaded if you click “Allow analytics”).

5. Analytics & cookies

Google Analytics is off by default. No Google Analytics script runs and no analytics cookies are set unless you click “Allow analytics” on the consent banner. If you allow analytics, we count high-level actions (like test starts, completions, and copy clicks) without ever sending your quiz answers, personality scores, search queries, or custom instruction text. Page query strings and URL fragments are excluded.

You can change or withdraw your choice anytime via Analytics preferences, which disables tracking immediately and clears first-party _ga cookies. Google also provides a browser opt-out add-on.

6. Retention & your rights

We never store your quiz answers or tool inputs. If you email us, we keep the thread only as long as needed to help you. You can ask to access, export, or delete any correspondence we hold by emailing hello@agent-tune.com. We honor GDPR, CCPA, and equivalent privacy rights for all visitors regardless of location.

7. Security, children & updates

All pages are served over HTTPS. AgentTune is not directed to children under 13. When we update this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page. Questions? Email hello@agent-tune.com or read our Terms of Service.
