What the research can tell you.

Our empirical studies examine 2,000+ scored AI questionnaire sessions across Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, and Claude Code, plus a 60-reply controlled before/after tuning pilot (and 180-reply evaluation protocol). They do not prove that AI has a human personality or that a template guarantees higher accuracy—every article publishes its raw response vectors, scoring scripts, and limits.
