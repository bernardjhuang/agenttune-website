Five small wins. Every conversation.

Tuning isn't about a personality test on the wall — it's about removing friction from every reply your agent gives.

#01

Less re-prompting

Stop typing "be more direct," "skip the recap," "no caveats." The model already knows.

#02

Faster answers

Cut 30–60% of the words you don't read anyway. Same depth, less noise.

#03

Fewer dead-ends

No more bullet menus when you wanted a recommendation. No more recommendations when you wanted options.

#04

Real pushback

If you're the type who wants challenge, you get challenge. Not a polite hedge wrapped in three disclaimers.

#05

Carries everywhere

Use the same file in Claude, GPT, Cursor, your own API call. Switch models, keep the tuning.

Models default to everyone. You are not everyone.

Frontier models are trained on the population, then RLHF'd toward the safest, most agreeable response anyone could want. That's a sensible default for billions of users — and a mediocre one for any individual.

A personality test is the cheapest known instrument for telling a model how you differ from the average. AgentTune is just the bridge: the test gives you a type, the type compiles to a Markdown file, the file becomes context the next time you press send.

No magic. No new model. Just the right context, written down once.
