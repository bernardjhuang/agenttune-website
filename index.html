Start with one clear request.

“Be more direct” can mean several things. Write down what you want to change: where the answer starts, how much detail you need, and when you want alternatives.

For example, ask for a recommendation followed by the evidence. Keep uncertainty visible when it affects the decision. You can request a different style in any later task.

What the research can tell you.

Our research examines AI responses to personality questionnaires. It does not prove that AI has a human personality or that a template improves performance. The articles publish methods, data and limitations so you can assess the evidence.
