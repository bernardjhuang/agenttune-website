What personality type is ChatGPT?

Reported questionnaire scores and their limits

The source report labels GPT-5.5 INTJ in 100 scoring results. That is a description of those reported outputs, not a diagnosis of ChatGPT or a claim about today's models.

What the counts mean

These are exploratory reports of model-generated self-descriptions, not a validated measure of AI personality or evidence that personality matching improves outcomes. The MBTI total includes repeated scoring of a single answer vector for GLM, Grok, and MiniMax; it does not represent 600 independent model responses. Protocols differed across models. See the methodology and limitations.

The original MBTI report lists Claude Opus 4.7 at 99/100 INTJ, GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro at 100/100, GLM 5.1 at 98/100, and Grok 4.3 and MiniMax 2.7 at 100/100. Independent-response counts are not consistently documented. The single-vector GLM method also conflicts with its two reported type labels; raw records are needed to resolve that.

Other reports use Big Five, DISC, attachment, and Enneagram questionnaires. Differences can reflect prompting, agent overlays, scoring, or model behavior. They do not establish a universal personality, explain training effects, or rank which instrument produces better tuning.

Questions people ask

What MBTI type is ChatGPT?

The source reports 100 INTJ scoring results for GPT-5.5. Its mixed procedure does not establish a stable personality for ChatGPT or an independent-response rate for current models.

What personality type is Claude?

The source reports 99 INTJ and one ISTJ result for Opus 4.7. The Opus 4.8 follow-up used synthetic sampling from one probability profile, so it cannot establish a version change.

Do all AI models have the same personality?

These reports cannot answer that. They summarize model-generated answers to human questionnaires, with different protocols, repeated scoring, and possible agent-overlay effects.

Can instructions change how an AI responds?

You can ask for a different level of detail, tone, or structure. Treat the templates as editable preferences and compare outputs on your own tasks; no controlled benefit from personality matching is established here.

Try preferences you can evaluate

Choose a tuning template, edit rules that do not fit, and compare the same task with and without it. Assess factual accuracy as well as tone. You can also use the compact instruction generator or explore the questionnaires.
