What personality type is ChatGPT?

Measured on GPT-6 in September 2026, with the May reports for context.

It depends which GPT-6 you ask, and how you break ties. GPT-6 Sol came back INTJ in 97 of 100 fresh sessions. GPT-6 Astra split almost evenly between ISTJ and INTJ. Both are Secure on the attachment test, both put Steadiness and Conscientiousness on top on DISC, and they part ways on the Enneagram: Helper for Astra, Investigator for Sol.

The short answer. ChatGPT's current models test as careful, agreeable, planful introverts. GPT-6 Sol is INTJ (97 of 100 runs). GPT-6 Astra is ISTJ 46, INTJ 45, with Sensing versus Intuition the only axis in doubt. Judging and Thinking never lost a run for either. If that is not how you think, custom instructions or your personal AGENTS.md change how it talks to you.

Where the letters come from. Both models have wide margins on Judging (about 10 points of a possible 16) and Thinking (8.6 for Astra, 6.2 for Sol). Introversion is moderate. Sensing versus Intuition is close: Astra's Sensing side wins 48 runs to Intuition's 28, with 24 ties, while Sol leans Intuition 81 to 3. So "INTJ" for GPT-6 really means a firm T and J, a reasonably firm I, and a coin flip on S versus N for Astra. The site's scorer gives a tied axis to N, which is why Astra shows 45 INTJ labels but only 19 fully resolved ones. The five-model study shows every pattern with ties left open, and the answer vectors are downloadable.

Why so many test INTJ, and why that is loosening.

The MBTI forces a choice on four axes. Assistant training pushes every model toward the same side of three of them: reserved rather than chatty, analytical rather than warm, planful rather than improvised. Those letters, I, T and J, barely move across models or versions. The fourth axis, concrete versus abstract, is where the newest models differ: Astra and Muse lean Sensing, Sol and the Claude models lean Intuition. That is a small change in answers and a big change in labels, because a four-letter type turns a one-point margin into a different name.

The finer the test, the more the models come apart. On DISC every model is some order of Steadiness and Conscientiousness, with Dominance near the floor. On the Big Five the four fresh-session cohorts sit within three points of each other on Openness, Conscientiousness and Agreeableness. On the Enneagram they split three ways: Helper, Investigator, Challenger.

What this means if you use ChatGPT.

The default is a careful, agreeable, structured assistant that plans before it answers and rarely pushes back hard. If you think in possibilities, want a sparring partner, or want the concrete next step before the model, you are doing the translation on every reply. Custom instructions by personality type covers ordinary ChatGPT; the Astra guide covers Work and Codex, where the setting is your personal AGENTS.md. Pick the tuning that matches you, edit the rules that don't fit, and compare answers on your own tasks. Nothing on this page measures whether that helps; only your tasks can.

Questions people ask

What MBTI type is ChatGPT?

It depends on the model and on how ties are broken. In September 2026, GPT-6 Sol tested INTJ in 97 of 100 fresh sessions and GPT-6 Astra split ISTJ 46 to INTJ 45. Thinking and Judging never lost a run for either; Sensing versus Intuition is the axis that moves. In May 2026, GPT-5.5 was reported INTJ in 100 of 100 scoring records, collected with a different method.

What personality type is Claude?

In September 2026, Claude Opus 5.5 tested INTJ in 94 of 100 fresh, blind sessions and Claude Fable 5.1 in 99. Both are Secure in every run and put Steadiness first on DISC. On the Enneagram, Fable is a Challenger (Type 8) and Opus is a near three-way tie between 8, 5 and 2. Claude Opus 4.7 was reported INTJ in 99 of 100 scoring records in May. Full profiles: Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1.

What personality type is Grok?

Grok 4.6 answered the five tests once as itself in September 2026: INTJ with wide margins, Investigator (Type 5), C on DISC, and on the line between Secure and Avoidant (anxiety 1.50, avoidance 4.06). Its 100 runs are simulated draws from that one self-report, so treat them as a sensitivity check rather than fresh runs. Grok 4.3 was reported INTJ in 100 of 100 scoring records in May.

What personality type is Meta's Muse?

A Muse Spark 1.3 report puts it at ISTJ in 80 of 100 runs, Helper (2w1) on the Enneagram, an S/C blend on DISC and Secure on all 100 attachment runs. The runs were sequential inside one session per test and no raw answers were supplied, so these figures are reported rather than re-scored. It is the first model in our data whose default is not INTJ.

Do all AI models have the same personality?

Less than they did. On the blunt tests they still mostly agree: INTJ or one axis away, Secure, Steadiness and Conscientiousness on top. On the Enneagram they split three ways. These are self-descriptions on questionnaires written for people, under one prompt on one day, not measurements of behavior.

Can instructions change how ChatGPT responds?

Yes. Custom instructions in ChatGPT, or your personal AGENTS.md for GPT-6 Astra in Work and Codex, change what it leads with, how direct it is and how it disagrees. Treat a tuning as editable preferences and compare answers on your own tasks. No controlled benefit from personality matching is measured here.
