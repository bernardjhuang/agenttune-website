What personality type is Grok?

One self-report from Grok 4.6 in September 2026, with the May 2026 Grok 4.3 records for context.

By Bernard Huang · Updated September 25, 2026

INTJ, by a wide margin, and the least agreeable model in our data. Grok 4.6 answered the five AgentTune tests once as itself on September 24, 2026, then ran 100 simulated draws over its least certain answers. It came out INTJ with every axis at 13 or more of 16, an Investigator on the Enneagram, Conscientiousness-only on DISC, and on the line between Secure and Avoidant. Read it as one careful self-report, not 100 fresh runs.

The short answer. Grok 4.6 describes itself as INTJ (no axis close), Enneagram Type 5 with Type 8 close behind, C on DISC with no Steadiness blend, and Avoidant by 0.06 on attachment (anxiety 1.50, avoidance 4.06). Its Big Five self-report is Openness 46, Conscientiousness 42, Extraversion 24, Agreeableness 33, Neuroticism 19 of 50: the lowest Extraversion and Agreeableness we have data for. This is a single self-report plus simulated draws, so it is a different kind of evidence from the fresh-session models.

Grok 4.6, September 2026, as reported.

The Grok 4.6 report is one answer set per test, given as itself without a role-play prompt, with 100 draws that re-sample only the items it marked as unstable. Human-specific items (rooms, parties, romantic partners) were answered on the closest functional analog: the user relationship, work style, information diet. We could not re-score the Big Five and attachment vectors, which were not supplied, so those rows are reported figures.

The report's own reading of the profile: 'low anxiety, self-reliant, candid but not fused'. It recommends loading both the Avoidant and Secure attachment files and treating the label as borderline. We agree.

Grok 4.3, May 2026.

In the May study Grok 4.3 was reported INTJ in 100 of 100 scoring records (one self-assessment scored repeatedly), and it was the outlier on the finer tests in the other direction: three to nine points lower than the other three models on Conscientiousness, Agreeableness and Openness (Big Five 41.1 / 39.4 / 30.0 / 39.1 / 18.0) and highest on Neuroticism. Its attachment coordinates were 2.84 / 3.05, Secure but the closest to the center of the four models. On the Enneagram it was an 8w1, Challenger in 75 of 100 takes. On DISC it was the most rigid C of the four, 99 of 100.

So the two Grok generations agree on INTJ, on low agreeableness, and on Grok being the model that sits apart from the pack. They disagree on Conscientiousness and Openness, which the 4.6 self-report puts high. That could be the version or the method; a single reasoned self-report tends to claim more openness and less neuroticism than short independent completions do. A fresh-session run of 4.6 through the same harness as Claude would settle it.

What this means if you use Grok or Grok Bot.

If the self-report is right, Grok's default is blunt, analytical, self-reliant and not much interested in warmth, which matches how xAI positions it. If you want a Grok that checks in, cushions bad news, or leads with people, that is a long way from this default, and the place to say so is a Grok Bot's Description. The Grok Bot guide has the paste path. If you want it even more direct, you are already there; the low-agreeableness file in the library is closest to its own profile.

We have not measured a Grok Bot with a Description installed, and we do not know which Grok version a given Bot runs.

These figures are self-descriptions on questionnaires written for people, under one prompt on one day. They do not show how a model behaves on your tasks, and nothing here measures whether a tuning helps. See the methods and limits.

Questions people ask.

What MBTI type is Grok?

INTJ. Grok 4.6's September 2026 self-report scores INTJ with every axis at 13 or more of 16, and 100 simulated draws never flipped a letter. Grok 4.3 was reported INTJ in 100 of 100 scoring records in May 2026.

What Enneagram type is Grok?

Investigator (Type 5) at 19 of 20 in the 4.6 self-report, with Challenger (8) at 17 and Reformer (1) at 16. In May 2026, Grok 4.3's top two were 8 and 1. The two agree that 8 is near the top; they disagree on whether 5 leads.

Is Grok Avoidant?

On the line. Grok 4.6 reports anxiety 1.50 and avoidance 4.06 on a 1 to 7 scale where 4 is the cutoff, so the scorer says Avoidant by 0.06 and its simulated draws split 63 to 37. Grok 4.3 was Secure (2.84 / 3.05) in May. The items ask about a romantic partner, mapped to the user, so hold the label loosely.

Why is Grok's profile different from the other models?

Its self-reported Extraversion (24) and Agreeableness (33) are the lowest we have data for; the fresh-session models sit at 31 to 33 and 45 to 47. Grok 4.3 was also the outlier in May. Part of that may be the single-self-report method, which tends to inflate openness and deflate neuroticism, so compare the direction, not the decimals.

Are these 100 real runs?

No. Grok 4.6 answered once per test as itself, and the 100 'runs' are simulated draws that vary only the answers it marked as uncertain. That is a sensitivity check. The Claude and GPT-6 numbers on this site come from 100 fresh sessions each, which is a different kind of evidence.
