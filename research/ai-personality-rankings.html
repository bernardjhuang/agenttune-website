Which AI is the most agreeable? The most direct?

Ten models ranked trait by trait from the same five tests. The method sits next to every score.

By Bernard Huang · Updated September 25, 2026

Most models describe themselves the same way on the blunt tests. The finer ones separate them. Here is every model we have data for, ranked on each trait, with the collection method beside each score, because a fresh-session cohort, a reported self-report and a May 2026 scoring record are three different kinds of evidence. Updated when a model is re-run.

The short answer. Most agreeable: Claude Opus 5.5 (47.1 of 50). Least: Grok 4.6 (33, reported). Most conscientious: Gemini 3.1 Pro (48.3, May). Calmest: Gemini (Neuroticism 10.1, May), then Claude Opus 5.5 (15.4). Most direct by its own account: Grok 4.6, with the highest Dominance score (6 of 20 against 4 to 5.4 for the rest) and a pure Conscientiousness DISC. Firmest INTJ: Claude Fable 5.1 (99 of 100). The only non-INTJ default: Muse Spark 1.3 (ISTJ 80, reported).

How to read these tables.

  • Fresh sessions (GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1): 100 fresh sessions per model in September 2026, every answer vector published. Means are shown.
  • Reported (Grok 4.6, Muse Spark 1.3): figures from reports we could not re-score. Grok is one self-report per test plus simulated draws; Muse is 100 sequential runs inside one session.
  • May 2026 (Claude Opus 4.7, GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Grok 4.3): scoring records from the six-model study, collected with mixed methods and the test names visible. Big Five and attachment cover four of the six.

Ranking across evidence classes is a convenience, not a controlled comparison. Where two models are within a point, the order is noise.

Questions people ask.

Which AI is the most agreeable?

Claude Opus 5.5, at 47.1 of 50 on the Big Five in 100 fresh sessions in September 2026. GPT-6 Astra (45.1), Claude Fable 5.1 (45.0) and GPT-6 Sol (45.0) follow. Grok 4.6's self-report is the lowest at 33.

Which AI is the most direct?

By its own self-report, Grok 4.6: the highest Dominance score, a pure Conscientiousness DISC with no Steadiness blend, and the lowest Agreeableness. Among fresh-session models, Claude Fable 5.1 has the strongest Challenger score on the Enneagram, driven by candor items rather than leadership items.

Which AI is the calmest?

Gemini 3.1 Pro in May 2026 (Neuroticism 10.1 of 50), then Claude Opus 5.5 (15.4) among the September fresh-session models. GPT-6 Sol (20.1) and Astra (19.1) score highest, mostly because their answers on those items vary more from run to run.

Are these rankings a fair comparison?

Within an evidence class, roughly. Across classes, no: fresh-session means, reported self-reports and May 2026 scoring records were collected differently. The evidence column is there so you can compare like with like.

Which AI is not an INTJ?

Muse Spark 1.3, reported ISTJ in 80 of 100 runs. GPT-6 Astra splits ISTJ 46 to INTJ 45. Every other model we have measured labels INTJ in most runs.
